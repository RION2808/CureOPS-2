Our project is an **AI-powered patient triage and hospital assistance platform** designed to help healthcare staff quickly assess and prioritize patients based on the urgency of their condition.

The platform collects important patient information such as symptoms, pain severity, oxygen levels, blood pressure, injuries, and other relevant indicators. Using these inputs, the system generates an **urgency score from 1–5**, helping medical staff identify cases that may require faster attention.

A centralized **hospital operations dashboard** allows nurses and staff to view incoming patients, their urgency levels, essential medical information, and current status. This provides a clear overview of the emergency workload and can help staff organize patient flow more efficiently.

The system is designed as a **decision-support tool rather than a replacement for doctors**. Final medical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals, while the platform helps organize information and highlight potentially urgent cases.

The project addresses a common challenge in emergency healthcare: when multiple patients arrive simultaneously, staff need to quickly understand who may require immediate attention. By structuring patient information and providing AI-assisted prioritization, our solution aims to reduce delays, improve workflow visibility, and support more efficient emergency management.

### Key Features

* AI-assisted patient urgency classification
* 1–5 urgency scoring system
* Patient registration and symptom collection
* Hospital staff operational dashboard
* Patient status tracking
* Centralized patient information
* Real-time overview of incoming cases
* Database-ready architecture

Our goal is to create a **simple, scalable, and practical healthcare technology solution** that supports medical teams in managing patient flow and prioritizing care more effectively.
